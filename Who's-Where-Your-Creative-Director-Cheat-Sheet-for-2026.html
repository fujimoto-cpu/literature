2025年を席巻した、めまぐるしいクリエイティブ・ディレクターのいす取りゲームは、まさに歴史に刻まれる一年だった。大手ファッションハウスへのデザイナーの加入・退任が年間を通じて業界ニュースを独占し、9月のファッション月間では、シャネルのマチュー・ブラジーからボッテガ・ヴェネタのルイーズ・トロッターまで、多くの新デザイナーがデビューを飾った。

2026年はこうした交代劇のペースが落ち着くと予想されるものの、まだいくつかのランウェイデビューへの期待と、次の動向が謎に包まれた”失業中”のデザイナーたちが残っている（オリヴィエ・ルスタン、ジョン・ガリアーノ、エディ・スリマン、サバト・デ・サルノ、ケイシー・キャドワラダー、ルーク＆ルーシー・メイエール、リカルド・ティッシなど）。そして誰もが気になる大きな疑問がある——ヴェルサーチェのダリオ・ヴィターレが一シーズン限りで退任した後、後任は誰になるのか？（掲載時点ではアライアのピーター・ミュリエルが最有力候補とされている。）

3.1 Phillip Lim

Michelle Rhee

創設者フィリップ・リムの退任後、ミシェル・リーを後継者に指名。2026年春コレクションで初コレクションを発表。

Area

Nicholas Aburn

2025年3月就任。以前はバレンシアガ・クチュールのシニアデザイナー。9月のNYFWで初コレクションを披露。

Balenciaga

Pierpaolo Piccioli

元ヴァレンティノ。グッチへ移籍したデムナの後任として2025年に就任。2026年春コレクションでデビュー。

Balmain

Antonin Tron

アトラン創設者。オリヴィエ・ルスタンのサプライズ退任後、2025年11月に任命。2026年秋のデビューコレクションは3月のパリで発表予定。

Carven

Mark Howard Thomas

内部昇格。ボッテガ・ヴェネタへ移籍したルイーズ・トロッターの後任として2025年3月に任命。2023年入社。2026年春コレクションでランウェイデビュー。

Christian Louboutin (Men’s)

Jaden Smith

2025年9月、ブランド初のメンズ・クリエイティブ・ディレクターに就任。パリに移住。シューズ・レザーグッズ・アクセサリーの年4コレクションを担当。キャンペーン・イベント・体験型コンテンツの開発も担う。

Dior

Jonathan Anderson

ウィメンズ・メンズ・オートクチュール全カテゴリを統括。クリスチャン・ディオール本人以来初。マリア・グラツィア・キウリ（ウィメンズ）とキム・ジョーンズ（メンズ）の後任として就任。2026年春コレクションでデビュー。

Fendi

Maria Grazia Chiuri

ディオール退任後、古巣フェンディにCCOとして復帰（1989年在籍歴あり）。シルヴィア・ヴェントゥリーニ・フェンディの後継。2026年秋ミラノで初コレクション。

Gucci

Demna

バレンシアガで10年間指揮を執った後、2025年7月に同じくケリング傘下のグッチへ移籍。サバト・デ・サルノの後任。

Hermès (Men’s)

Grace Wales Bonner

英国人デザイナー、受賞歴のある自身のブランドも主宰。デビューコレクションは2027年1月のメンズ・ファッション・ウィーク。

Jean Paul Gaultier

Duran Lantink

引退発表から5年後、初の常設CD（RTW＋クチュール）に就任。2025年9月にパリFWで初RTWを発表。初クチュールは2026年1月。

Jil Sander

Simone Belotti

元バリーCD。ルーシー＆ルーク・メイエールの後任として、OTBグループが発表。2026年春コレクションでデビュー。

Loewe

Jack McCollough and Lazaro Hernandez

プロエンザ・スクーラー共同創設者。ジョナサン・アンダーソンの後任として2025年3月に就任。2025年9月に初コレクションを発表。

Maison Margiela

Glenn Martens

Y/Project出身、ディーゼルと兼任。2025年1月にジョン・ガリアーノの後任として発表。2025年9月にデビューランウェイ。

Marni

Meryll Rogge

ベルギー人デザイナー、自身のブランド（2020年〜）も主宰。創設者コンスエロ・カスティリオーニ以来初の女性CD。2026年初頭にデビュー予定。

Mugler

Miguel Castro Freitas

ポルトガル人、セントラル・セント・マーティンズ卒。ディオール・サンローラン・ドリス・ヴァン・ノッテン経験者。ケイシー・キャドワラダーの後任。2026年春パリFWでデビュー。

Proenza Schouler

Rachel Scott

ディオティマのデザイナー。ロエベへ移籍した創業者2人（ジャック・マコロー＆ラザロ・エルナンデス）の後任。2026年2月に初の本格コレクションを披露予定。

📄 原文

The head-spinning game of creative director musical chairs that played out across 2025 was one for the history books. An ongoing series of designers exiting and joining major fashion houses dominated industry headlines all year long, culminating in a September fashion month season full of new designers’ debuts, from Matthieu Blazy at Chanel to Louise Trotter at Bottega Veneta.

While the pace of these switch-ups is expected to slow down in 2026, there are still more runway debuts to anticipate, and several recently-unemployed designers whose next moves remain a mystery (Olivier Rousteing, John Galliano, Hedi Slimane, Sabato De Sarno, Casey Cadwallader, Luke and Lucie Meier and Riccardo Tisci, to name a handful). And then there’s the big question on everyone’s mind as we enter the new year: Who will replace Dario Vitale at Versace following his season-long stint? (At press time, Alaïa’s Pieter Mulier is the top rumor.) And what kind of designer domino effect will that replacement set off?

To help you stay on top of these comings and goings as the Fall 2026 season approaches, we’re keeping track via the guide below, which we’ll update continuously as new announcements are made throughout the year. To start, it includes every major appointment made since Jan. 1, 2025 by brand, in alphabetical order.

3.1 Phillip Lim — Following founder Phillip Lim’s departure, the brand named Michelle Rhee as his successor. She showed her first collection for Spring 2026.

Area — Nicholas Aburn joined Area as creative director in March 2025 and showed his first collection for the brand at NYFW in September. He had previously been a senior designer at Balenciaga Couture.

Balenciaga — Balenciaga tapped Pierpaolo Piccioli as its new creative director in 2025. The Valentino alum replaced Demna (Gucci’s new artistic director), making his Balenciaga debut for Spring 2026.

Balmain — Following Olivier Rousteing’s surprise exit from Balmain, the French maison announced Antonin Tron as its new creative director in November 2025. The Atlein founder will present his debut Balmain collection in Paris in March for Fall 2026

Carven — Carven promoted from within to replace Louise Trotter after her departure to Bottega Veneta, announcing in March 2025 that Mark Howard Thomas would fill the role of director of design. He joined the house in 2023 as head of sartorial and creative collaborations, and made his runway debut for Spring 2026.

Christian Louboutin (Men’s) — In September 2025, Christian Louboutin named Jaden Smith as the label’s first men’s creative director. Smith is moving to Paris to assume the role, and will present his first designs in January during men’s fashion week. He will oversee the creation of four collections a year across shoes, leather goods and accessories, along with developing campaigns, events and immersive experiences.

Dior — Last year, Jonathan Anderson succeeded Maria Grazia Chiuri (who oversaw women’s) and Kim Jones (who helmed men’s) as Dior ‘s creative director. He’s the first since Christian Dior himself to work across all women’s, men’s and haute couture collections. His debuted his first men’s and women’s collections for Spring 2026.

Fendi — After Maria Grazia Chiuri exited her role as Dior ‘s creative director of women’s, Fendi announced in October 2025 that she would be returning to the Italian house as its chief creative officer. (She worked there as a designer in 1989 before joining Valentino.) Chiuri succeeds Silvia Venturini Fendi, who had recently stepped down and assumed the role of honorary president. Chiuri will debut her first collection for Fendi in Milan in February for Fall 2026.

Gucci — After 10 years at the helm of Balenciaga, Demna officially joined fellow Kering -owned brand Gucci as artistic director in July 2025. He succeeded Sabato De Sarno, who left after two years in the role.

Hermès (Men’s) — Last fall, Hermès appointed a new menswear creative director: British designer Grace Wales Bonner, who also runs an award-winning namesake fashion line. She will present her debut Hermès Men’s collection in January 2027.

Jean Paul Gaultier — Five years after designer Jean Paul Gaultier announced his retirement from fashion, the brand tapped emerging designer Duran Lantink as its permanent creative director, helming both ready-to-wear and couture. He presented his debut ready-to-wear collection during Paris Fashion Week in September 2025, while his first couture show is scheduled for January 2026.

Jil Sander — Not long after Lucie and Luke Meier unveiled their final collection for Jil Sander last February, the house and its parent company OTB Group announced their successor: Simone Belotti. Previously the creative director of Bally, Belotti showed his first Jil Sander collection for Spring 2026.

Loewe — After leaving the brand they co-founded, Proenza Schouler, Jack McCollough and Lazaro Hernandez were named creative directors at Loewe in March 2025, replacing Jonathan Anderson. They showed their first collection for the house in September 2025.

Maison Margiela — In January 2025, Maison Margiela and parent company OTB Group confirmed that Glenn Martens would replace John Galliano as creative director. Martens continues to helm Diesel (also owned by OTB) and previously designed his own brand Y/Project, which he left in 2024. His debut Maison Margiela runway took place in September 2025.

Marni — Marni announced in July 2025 that Belgian designer Meryll Rogge would be its next creative director, replacing Francesco Risso. She’s the first woman to helm the brand since founder Consuelo Castiglioni. Rogge also operates her own eponymous brand, which she launched in 2020. She’ll make her Marni runway debut in early 2026.

Mugler — In April 2025, Portugese designer Miguel Castro Freitas officially replaced Casey Cadwallader as creative director of Mugler. The Central Saint Martins graduate and alum of Dior, Saint Laurent and Dries Van Noten showed his debut collection for the brand during Spring 2026 Paris Fashion Week.

Proenza Schouler — Proenza Schouler announced in September 2025 that Diotima designer Rachel Scott had replaced founders Jack McCollough and Lazaro Hernandez (who’d decamped to Loewe) as creative director. After a September preview, she’ll showcase her first full collection for the label in February 2026.
