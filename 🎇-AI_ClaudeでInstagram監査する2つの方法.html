How to audit your Instagram with Claude

BEFORE YOU START — BOTH OPTIONS

Switch Instagram to a Professional account

  • Personal Instagram accounts have no API access and no Insights dashboard. You need a Business or Creator account for either method to work properly. The switch is free, instant, and reversible — you lose nothing.
  • Open Instagram → tap your profile picture → hamburger menu (≡) → Settings and privacy
  • Account type and tools → Switch to Professional account
  • Choose Business (brands, stores) or Creator (influencers, content creators) → pick a category → done

Followers, posts, username, and engagement history are all preserved. Takes about 3 minutes.

OPTION A — CONNECTOR METHOD

What you need for the Windsor.ai connector

Instagram Business or Creator account Paid Claude plan Windsor.ai account

  • Windsor.ai is a verified third-party connector listed in Claude’s Connectors Directory. It’s the bridge between Instagram’s Graph API and Claude. Windsor has a Forever Free plan (1 data source, 1 account, 30-day data history limit) and a 30-day trial (10 sources, 15 accounts, full history). For serious audits, a paid plan gives you more history and accounts.
  • Connectors require a paid Claude plan — Pro (20/mo),Max(100/mo), or Team ($25/user/mo). Free accounts can browse the directory but can’t connect.
  • Setup is no-code and takes under 2 minutes once you have a Windsor account.

OPTION A — STEP 1

Connect Instagram to Windsor.ai

  • Go to onboard.windsor.ai and create an account — the 30-day trial requires no credit card
  • Click Add data source → search for Instagram Insights (your own account analytics) or Instagram Public (any public profile)
  • Click Authenticate via Facebook — log in with the Facebook account linked to your Instagram
  • Select the Instagram profile(s) you want to analyze and confirm. Windsor syncs your data automatically.

Windsor normalizes raw API data into clean, AI-readable format so Claude doesn’t misinterpret metrics.

スライド 4

Claude

OPTION A — STEP 2

Add Windsor to Claude

  • Open claude.ai in a browser or desktop app — connectors can only be added on web or desktop, not the mobile app
  • Click the ”+” button in the lower left of any chat → Connectors → search Windsor.ai → click Connect
  • Sign in with your Windsor account when prompted to authorize the connection
  • Set permissions to “Always allow” — skips re-authorization in every new chat

Once set up on web, the connector is available when you log in on mobile too.

OPTION A — STEP 3

Run your audit — connector prompts

  • Start a new chat, enable Windsor via ”+”, and ask in plain English. Data streams live from Instagram’s API — no exports needed.

“Audit my Instagram for the last 30 days. What’s my engagement rate, top posts, and follower trend?”

“Which content type gets the most saves — Reels, carousels, or photos?”

“What time of day are my followers most active? When should I post?”

“Give me 3 recommendations to improve reach based on my last 60 days.”

“Compare my Reel average watch time vs. my posting frequency this month.”

OPTION B — CLAUDE IN CHROME

What you need for the browser method

Google Chrome or Edge Paid Claude plan Instagram logged in

  • No third-party account needed — Claude uses your existing logged-in Chrome session to browse Instagram and Meta Business Suite directly.
  • Chrome or Edge only — not supported on Brave, Arc, Firefox, or Safari.
  • Model note: Pro plan is limited to Haiku 4.5. Max, Team, and Enterprise can choose Sonnet 4.6 (best for complex audits) or Opus 4.6.

OPTION B — STEP 1

Install Claude in Chrome

  • Go to the Chrome Web Store → search “Claude” → find the extension published by Anthropic → click Add to Chrome
  • Accept the permissions when prompted — Claude needs them to click, navigate, and take screenshots
  • Click the puzzle piece icon in your toolbar → find Claude → click the thumbtack to pin it
  • Sign in with your Claude account when prompted
  • In claude.ai → Settings → Connectors → find Claude in Chrome → toggle it on

スライド 6

Claude

OPTION B — STEP 2

Enable Chrome in your chat and pick your model

  • Start a new Claude chat → click the ”+” button in the lower left → hover Connectors → toggle Claude in Chrome

This is off by default for each conversation — you must enable it each time, or set a default in the extension settings.

  • If on Max, Team, or Enterprise: click the model selector → choose Sonnet 4.6 for complex multi-step audits, or Opus 4.6 for reasoning tasks.

Pro plan users are fixed to Haiku 4.5 — there is no way to change this on Pro.

  • Log in to Instagram in Chrome (and business.facebook.com for the full Insights dashboard)

Browser automation uses more of your Claude usage allowance than regular chat — heavier users should consider Max over Pro.

OPTION B — STEP 3

Run your audit — Chrome prompts

  • Type your request. Claude navigates your browser, reads the screen, and reports back. When asked to access instagram.com, click Always allow actions on this site.

“Go to instagram.com/[yourhandle]. Read my last 12 posts and summarize my content mix, engagement, and caption style.”

“Navigate to business.facebook.com, open my Instagram Insights, and give me a plain-English summary of what you see.”

“Open my profile and my top competitor’s profile in two tabs. Compare posting frequency and visible engagement.”

“Browse my last 20 posts. Which ones have the most likes and comments? Build a quick table.”

SUMMARY

Which option should you use?

  • Choose Option A (connector) if you want real structured data — saves rate, watch time, story exits, audience demographics, follower growth trends. Best for recurring marketing analysis. Needs Windsor.ai account.
  • Choose Option B (Chrome) if you want a quick visual audit with zero extra setup. Claude browses your profile and Meta Business Suite and reads what’s on screen. No third-party account needed.
  • Using both together is also valid — Chrome for a quick qualitative read, connector for the deep numbers dive.
  • On the free Claude plan? Export a CSV from Meta Business Suite → upload to Claude chat → ask Claude to analyze it. No paid plan required for this fallback.

💡 要約

🎯 一言要約 — ClaudeでInstagramアカウントを本格監査する2つの方法（Windsor.aiコネクタ / Claude in Chrome）を、ステップバイステップで解説したガイド。

🔌 Option A — Windsor.aiコネクタ方式

Windsor.aiを経由してInstagram Graph APIに接続し、Claudeからライブデータを分析。エンゲージメント率・保存率・リーチ・ストーリー離脱・視聴時間・フォロワー属性など深いメトリクスが取れる。30日間無料トライアルあり。セットアップ: Windsor登録→Instagram認証→Claudeのコネクタで接続→プロンプトで分析。

🌐 Option B — Claude in Chrome方式

Chrome拡張のClaude in Chromeを使い、ログイン中のInstagram画面をそのまま読み取る。追加アカウント不要。プロフィール・投稿グリッド・インサイト画面・競合プロフィールを閲覧可能。ただしバックエンドデータ（視聴時間・ストーリー離脱等）は取得不可。

📊 取得できるデータ比較

コネクタ（Option A）: Posts（いいね・コメント・保存・シェア・リーチ・インプレッション）、Reels（再生時間・リプレイ）、Stories（閲覧・返信・離脱・ナビタップ）、Audience（フォロワー成長・地域・アクティブ時間）、Competitors（公開プロフィール指標） Chrome（Option B）: プロフィールページ・投稿グリッド・インサイトダッシュボード・競合プロフィール（画面に表示されている情報のみ）

🎯 使い分けガイド

  • 定期的な数値分析が必要 → Option A（コネクタ）
  • サクッと定性的にチェックしたい → Option B（Chrome）
  • 両方組み合わせも有効（Chromeで定性、コネクタで定量）
  • 無料プランの代替: Meta Business SuiteからCSVエクスポート→Claudeにアップロード

💡 ゆりこの活用メモ

  • 社内SNS運用の提案ネタ: Windsor.ai + Claudeの組み合わせは「AI活用でSNS分析を自動化」の具体事例として、クライアント提案や社内研修で使える
  • Claude in Chromeで競合分析: 追加コストゼロで競合のInstagramを定性分析できるので、デザイン案件の市場調査フェーズに即導入可能
